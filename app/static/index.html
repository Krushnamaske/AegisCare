Evaluator quick demo — follow these steps
  1. Sign in as Citizen and submit a sample incident near Pune. Use the default coordinates or select “Use my location”.
  2. Open a second browser/incognito window and sign in as Hospital. Review the incident and pre-alert, then update facility resources.
  3. Open a third browser/incognito window and sign in as Ambulance. Accept the pending dispatch request.
  4. Advance the dispatch through En route → Arrived → Transporting → Completed.
  5. Return to the Citizen/Admin dashboard and refresh to see the shared incident and dispatch status.

For the clearest demonstration, keep the three role sessions open in separate browser profiles. Live dashboard events use WebSockets; refresh is available as a fallback.

Demo accounts and fictional sample facilities are created automatically on first start. See SUBMISSION_GUIDE.md for the evaluator walkthrough.
